Healthy Food Every Day

RCCDP prioritizes the health and well-being of every child through policies that encourage healthy eating habits and teach the importance of good nutrition and daily exercise.

We provide breakfast, lunch, and an afternoon snack daily. Our food program operates in accordance with USDA Child and Adult Day Care Food Program guidelines.
