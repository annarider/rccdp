Privacy Policy

How rccdp.org collects and uses your information.

Organization: Redwood City Child Development Program
Website: rccdp.org

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is collected, used, and shared when you visit rccdp.org (the "Site").

What We Collect and Why

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, time zone, and cookies. We also collect information about the pages you view and how you interact with the Site. We refer to this as "Device Information."

We collect Device Information using:

  • Cookies — data files placed on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
  • Log files — which track actions on the Site (IP address, browser type, referring pages, date/time stamps).
  • Web beacons, tags, and pixels — electronic files used to record browsing information.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site. You can opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. Learn how Google uses your data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact Form

If you contact us through our website form, we collect the information you provide (name, email, phone, message). This information is used to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Screen for potential risk and fraud (IP address)
  • Understand how visitors use the Site (analytics)
  • Respond to contact form submissions
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

Sharing Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third-party organizations, except as required by law (e.g., in response to a subpoena or lawful request).

Your Rights

If you have submitted a contact form or provided personal information, you may request a copy of your data or ask that it be deleted. Contact us at info@rccdp.org.

If you are a European resident, you have the right to access, correct, update, or delete personal information we hold about you. Note that your information may be processed outside of Europe, including in the United States.

Children

This Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Do Not Track

We do not alter our data collection practices based on Do Not Track browser signals.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes to our practices or for legal or operational reasons. Updates will be posted on this page.
