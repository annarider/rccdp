Curriculum

Learning through discovery and play — in a safe, supportive environment.

Our Approach

We believe children learn best through their interactions with real materials, children, and adults in a safe and supportive environment. School readiness is deeply connected to children's social and emotional development.

Each program uses a blend of child-directed and teacher-guided activities — active and quiet, individual and group — engaging visual, auditory, tactile, and kinesthetic pathways.

Zoo Phonics

RCCDP incorporates the Zoo Phonics® curriculum — a multisensory language program that supports phonics, pre-literacy, and kindergarten readiness skills through movement, sound, and imagery.

Music Together® in Schools

Our Music Together® curriculum provides developmentally appropriate music education, supporting language, cognitive, emotional, social, and physical development — and enhancing family engagement.

Programs by Age Group

🌱

Infants & Toddler Program

Ages 11 months – 2 years

We promote self-esteem, security, and social competence through socialization. The primary focus encourages exploration of new environments and comfort with caregivers, while supporting developmental milestones.

We use the California Desired Results Developmental Profile to track each child's individual development.

🧩

Twos Program

Ages 2–3 years

Goals center on developing empathy and promoting self-identity. Children learn to recognize their own feelings and those of peers. Teachers expand vocabulary and help children express their needs. Children also develop self-help and independence skills, including potty training, handwashing, and dressing.

🎨

Preschool Program

Ages 3–4 years

This program recognizes the incredible growth of language, cognitive, social-emotional, and motor development during the first five years. We provide balanced activities — both child-directed and teacher-led, active and quiet — using multi-sensory instruction.

Zoo Phonics curriculum is incorporated to support phonics and pre-literacy skills.

⭐

Kindergarten Readiness

Ages 4–5 years

Small group activities for older preschoolers focus on phonics, writing first and last names, self-help skills, math, and science. The Zoo Phonics system — a multisensory language program — is central to this program's approach.

California Desired Results System

The California Department of Education mandates this approach. RCCDP completes Desired Results Developmental Profile assessments within 60 days of enrollment and every six months thereafter.

We also use the Environmental Rating Scale yearly and administer annual parent surveys.

The Six Desired Results

  • Children are personally and socially competent
  • Children are effective learners
  • Children show physical and motor competence
  • Children are safe and healthy
  • Families support their children's learning and development
  • Families achieve their goals
