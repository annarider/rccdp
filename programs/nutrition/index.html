Nutrition Programs

Healthy food every day — supporting growing minds and bodies.

Our Commitment to Healthy Eating

RCCDP prioritizes the health and well-being of the children in our care through policies that encourage healthy eating habits and teach the importance of good nutrition and daily exercise.

We incorporate cooking activities into our curriculum — bringing nutrition education directly into the classroom.

Our food program operates in accordance with USDA Child and Adult Day Care Food Program guidelines, and we receive reimbursements for maintaining high nutritional standards.
