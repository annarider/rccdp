Board of Directors

Dedicated community leaders guiding RCCDP's mission and growth.

Officers

Rafael Avendaño
President

Rafael joined the RCCDP Board in 2023. He is Executive Director of Redwood City Together with over 12 years of nonprofit leadership experience. He previously served on the Sequoia Healthcare District board and the North Fair Oaks Community Council. Rafael believes that providing young children opportunities to learn, play, and grow in supportive environments builds healthier, safer communities.

Patricia E. Hart
Vice President

Pat has been a board member since 2005. She worked as in-house counsel at Silicon Valley companies and holds a B.A. in History from UC Santa Barbara and a J.D. from University of the Pacific, McGeorge School of Law. She strongly emphasizes education's importance for kindergarten readiness.

Sherry Warp
Secretary

Board member since 2009 with 30 years of special education experience in public schools. Sherry is a Speech/Language Pathologist and Program Specialist holding an M.A. from San Diego State University. She has volunteered to screen preschoolers' vision since 1996, including screening RCCDP children annually.

Glenn Snyder
Treasurer

Glenn has over 20 years of FP&A and 8 years of corporate strategy experience at Franklin Templeton, Visa, Charles Schwab, Digital Realty, and Vacasa. He teaches Managerial Finance at SF State University and has published a novel and finance materials. B.A. from UCLA; M.B.A. from University of San Francisco.

Kiri Fluetsch
Executive Director

Kiri holds an M.A. in Early Childhood Education from St. Mary's College, with a thesis focused on Fine Motor Development. She advanced from teacher's aide to lead teacher and now serves as Executive Director. Both her son and daughter attend RCCDP.

Board Members

Hank Scherf
Board Member (since 1989)

Certified Financial Planner with MassMutual. Former vice president of sales at Silicon Valley startups and a retired Navy Captain. Serves on the Hiller Aviation Museum and Sequoia Hospital Foundation boards. B.S. Aerospace Engineering, M.S. Mechanical Engineering, M.B.A. from Stanford.

Kathy Schmidt
Board Member (since 2000)

Architect with a career spanning architecture and business. Recently retired from Real Estate Development with Eden Housing, Inc., a nonprofit affordable housing developer. B.A., Master of Architecture, and M.B.A. from Stanford University.

Efrat Shimoni
Board Member (since 2019)

Finance executive with 20+ years of technology leadership experience. Parent of a child with special needs, Efrat observed first-hand the life-changing benefits early childhood education can provide — which drew her to serve on RCCDP's board.

Michelle Williams
Board Member (since 2025)

Founder and CEO of a Silicon Valley startup merging entertainment and education through an animated STEM series. Former elementary school teacher and tenured Associate Professor at Michigan State University. Received the NSF Early Career Award. Ph.D. in Education from UC Berkeley; B.B.A. from University of Texas at Austin.
