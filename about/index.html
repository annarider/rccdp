It's All About the Kids!

Our mission, philosophy, and the families we serve.

Our Mission

The Redwood City Child Development Program, an early learning center primarily serving low-income families, will:

  • Provide a comprehensive education program helping children's successful integration and participation in their families, schools, and communities.
  • Provide a safe, healthy, child-centered learning environment based on the developmental needs of children.
  • Focus on the emotional, social, cognitive, and physical needs of every child as an individual and as a member of the community.
  • Encourage families and the community to take an active role in their children's learning and development.

Our Philosophy

We provide a safe, nurturing, and stimulating environment that contributes to the development of children, helping to meet their emotional, social, cognitive, and physical needs.

Our commitment is to guide and encourage each child's individual potential and support the involvement of families.

Parent Involvement

The RCCDP offers parent workshops and informational meetings on a regular basis throughout the year. We conduct annual surveys to determine which topics are of most interest to our parents.

Our program has a Parent Advisory Council (PAC) — a group of parents who come together monthly to provide support and advice to one another and to the program. The PAC organizes several social events and fundraisers throughout the year.

Social Services

Our staff supports parents with referrals to community agencies for:

  • Child care
  • Speech and language services
  • Family counseling
  • Immigration assistance
  • Food programs
  • Housing and employment
  • Job training

Our Children's Stories

Every child at RCCDP has a unique journey. Here are a few of the families we've had the privilege of supporting.

James's Story

A single teenage mother enrolled her infant son at RCCDP while working part-time and completing high school. James graduated from the program in summer 2018 and is now thriving in kindergarten — he loves art, basketball, and track.

Isabel's Story

Isabel attended RCCDP since age two. When her mother faced financial hardship, RCCDP provided a scholarship so she could complete preschool. Isabel now identifies advanced mathematical shapes and can spell both her first and last name.

Isaac's Story

Isaac joined RCCDP in spring 2018 at 13 months. When his language skills were lagging at age two, staff referred his family to Golden Gate Regional Center, where he now receives free speech therapy. He's communicating in 3–4 word sentences.

Lucy's Story

Lucy has attended since 18 months. After struggling at age three, her mother was connected with Fair Oaks Community Center services. Lucy is now thriving in Pre-K — she's mastered the Zoo Phonics reading program and is preparing for kindergarten.

Jazmin's Story

Jazmin joined as a toddler, raised by a single grandparent. After suffering febrile seizures as an infant and experiencing speech delays, she was supported through Golden Gate Regional Center. Now three years old, Jazmin has typical language development.
