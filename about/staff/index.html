Management & Staff

Our dedicated, multilingual team of early childhood professionals.

Our multilingual and multicultural staff consists of dedicated and highly qualified educators in the field of Early Childhood. All teachers hold California State Child Development Permits; teaching assistants have taken courses in ECE working toward their permits. All staff are trained in CPR and First Aid.
